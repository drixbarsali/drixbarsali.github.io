Today I am not talking about team health, but I challenge you — whatever goal you are pursuing:

A JOB?  
A SALE?  
A PROJECT?  
AN IDEA?

... I challenge you to receive 100 NOs.

This is the provocation I made to the FELLOW I was mentoring. I have been, and hope to continue being, a mentor (for the upcoming BOOSTs) for Sistech. My "fellow" is a refugee woman who is trying to change her life; a person who, after experiencing the worst, had the courage to start over, learning a new language and a new culture, far from her relatives and the country to which she had dedicated part of her life.

Courage. Many do not understand that you are not born with courage: you acquire it. Life sometimes beats you down to the point that the only thing you have left is that organ pumping blood. And you only remember to breathe. For some, the cruelty of life pushes towards two paths: letting go and giving up, or moving forward. That moving forward is what we call courage.

It is nothing more than small decisions to be made in a universe that, for some, is 0 or 1. Here it is: these people were forced to leave their lives behind, they didn't give up, but now the challenge is another: finding a role in the development world.

And it is on this that I challenged her to get 100 NOs. In her case, I lowered the stakes to "10"; in my opinion, they were enough. Maybe it sounds crazy, but I challenge you, dear reader: 100 business proposals, 100 CVs sent... whatever it is, I challenge you to receive 100 NOs.

I too, when I decided to drop everything, carried this challenge in my pocket! Of course, it is not comparable to a "fellow" who had to change everything not by choice, but the challenge made me laugh more than cry. I wanted to try and prove that the rule of failure is nonsense.

With every NO I received, I would say to myself: "only 99, 98, 97 to go...". In fact, I found my first job here in Italy and I still had a good 84 NOs left over!

I want to deeply thank Sistech for this wonderful opportunity to be a support and a guide in the Italian working world, of which I have been a part for 7 years now.

Thank you Bianca, Liuba, Fellows, Mentors, SheTech, and all the people I came into contact with. Thank you, Sistech. Thank you from the bottom of my heart.

Girl Power!

And you, how many NOs! do you have left to get what you are looking for?
