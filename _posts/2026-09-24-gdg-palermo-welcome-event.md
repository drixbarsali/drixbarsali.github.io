### Event Details

- **Event**: GDG Palermo Welcome Event

- **Speaker**: Adriano Barsali (Team Leader & Developer)
- **Topic**: Yohooo! – The art of being less of a developer and more of a pirate (Even with less rum and more coffee)

### Key Agenda Highlights

#### Yohooo! – The art of being less of a developer and more of a pirate (Even with less rum and more coffee) 🏴‍☠️

What can pirates teach us about teamwork in software development? Much more than Hollywood clichés suggest. Adriano will guide us through the discovery of pirate organizations: networks based on collaboration, shared rules, and mutual aid.

We will explore fundamental topics for anyone writing code in a team:

- ⚓ **Psychological Safety**: Creating an environment where asking for help is not seen as a weakness.
- ⚓ **Junior and Senior**: Overcoming the expectation that those with more experience must always have all the answers.
- ⚓ **Ego**: Remembering that a team's value is not measured by individual commits, but by the team's ability to move forward together.

The goal? Understanding how to best use our skills within a team.
