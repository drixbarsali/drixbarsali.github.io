### Key Agenda Highlights

#### Yohooo! – The Art of Being Less Developer and More Pirate (Though with Less Rum and More Coffee) 🏴‍☠️

What can pirates teach us about teamwork and engineering culture in software development? Much more than Hollywood clichés suggest. Historically, pirate crews were remarkably sophisticated organizations based on voluntary cooperation, shared rules (the Pirate Code), mutual accountability, and collective empowerment.

Starting from their code, this session tackles one of the toughest challenges engineering teams face: **ego**.

We will explore core principles essential for anyone building software in a team:

- ⚓ **Psychological Safety**: How to foster an environment where asking questions, admitting mistakes, or requesting help is celebrated as a strength rather than perceived as weakness.
- ⚓ **Junior & Senior Dynamics**: Overcoming imposter syndrome and breaking the myth that senior engineers must have all the answers.
- ⚓ **True Collaboration**: Shifting mindset from isolated individual contributors to a united crew sharing responsibility, knowledge, and shared goals.
- ⚓ **Taming the Ego**: Why a team's true velocity and value are never measured by a single commit, but by the crew's ability to navigate the storm and ship together.

The goal isn't to be less technically skilled—it's understanding how to harness our skills to lift up the entire crew.

Less ego, more crew. And definitely plenty of coffee. Yohooo! 🏴‍☠️
